# UAE Retail Sales Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project exploring retail sales performance in the UAE using **Microsoft Excel, MySQL, SQL, and Power BI**.

## Project Overview

This project analyses a synthetic UAE retail sales dataset covering January to December 2025. The objective is to identify sales trends, evaluate profitability, understand customer purchasing patterns, and generate meaningful business insights through data analysis and interactive visualisations.

The project demonstrates a complete data analytics workflow, from data preparation and SQL-based analysis to dashboard development and reporting.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** – Data inspection, cleaning, formatting, and preliminary analysis.
- **MySQL** – Database creation, data storage, and management.
- **SQL** – Data querying, aggregation, joins, CTEs, ranking, and time-based analysis.
- **Microsoft Power BI** – Data visualisation and interactive dashboard development.

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains 1,500 synthetic sales transactions for the period January–December 2025.

It includes the following information:

- Order details and order dates
- Customer and product information
- City and sales channel
- Product quantity and unit price
- Discounts, revenue, cost, and profit
- Payment methods and order statuses

**Important:** This is a synthetic dataset created for educational and portfolio purposes. It does not represent actual sales transactions or confidential data from a UAE retail company.

## Analysis Performed

The project includes the following analyses:

1. **Overall Sales Performance** – Total orders, revenue, cost, profit, and profit margin.
2. **City Analysis** – Comparison of sales and profitability across cities.
3. **Product Category Analysis** – Revenue and profit performance by product category.
4. **Monthly Sales Analysis** – Monthly revenue and profit trends.
5. **Customer Analysis** – Customer purchasing patterns and revenue contribution.
6. **Sales Channel Analysis** – Comparison of sales channels.
7. **Discount Impact Analysis** – Relationship between discount levels and profitability.
8. **Top Product Analysis** – Identification of the highest-revenue products.
9. **Monthly Growth Analysis** – Month-over-month revenue growth.

The main sales performance analysis focuses on delivered orders, with other order statuses retained for separate checks.
